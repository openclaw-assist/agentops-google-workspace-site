AgentOps Google Workspace Connector

This connector lets Chris Tran's locally operated AgentOps assistant perform explicitly authorized Google Workspace tasks such as managing email, calendar events, documents, spreadsheets, and files.

Not a public service. This integration is operated for one account owner and is not offered for general sign-up or third-party use.

What it does

  • Reads, organizes, drafts, or sends Gmail messages when requested or through owner-approved automations.
  • Reads and manages Calendar events.
  • Works with authorized Drive files, Docs, and Sheets.
  • Uses read-only contact access when needed to identify intended recipients.

How authorization works

Google OAuth is used so the account owner can review and approve each requested permission. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this connector or its use of Google data may be sent to chris.agentops@gmail.com.
