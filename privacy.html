Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

This policy explains how the AgentOps Google Workspace Connector accesses and uses Google user data. The connector is a private, single-user automation operated by Chris Tran and is not offered as a public service.

Google data accessed

Only after the Google account owner grants OAuth permission, the connector may access:

  • Gmail messages, metadata, labels, drafts, and sending functions;
  • Google Calendar events and calendars;
  • Google Drive files and metadata;
  • Google Docs and Google Sheets content;
  • Google Contacts information on a read-only basis.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to perform tasks requested by the account owner or automations the owner has explicitly approved. Examples include searching or organizing email, drafting or sending a requested message, creating or reviewing a calendar event, and reading or updating an authorized document or spreadsheet.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the account owner's controlled computer. Task outputs and project records may also be stored locally in the owner's AgentOps workspace. Access is limited to the account owner and the locally operated assistant environment. Reasonable operating-system access controls and secret-redaction practices are used to protect credentials and local records.

Service providers and disclosure

The connector does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make it available to data brokers. When necessary to complete an owner-requested task, relevant content may be processed by configured infrastructure or AI service providers acting as service providers for that task. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security of the account and system.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the owner's requested workflows, local project records, backups, or legal obligations. The account owner controls local retention and may delete locally stored outputs or revoke Google authorization at any time. Revoking authorization prevents future access but does not automatically delete records the owner intentionally saved outside Google.

Limited use

The connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

The account owner may review or revoke access from Google Account security settings and may request deletion of locally stored connector data. Because this is a single-user private integration, no independent third-party user accounts are supported.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to chris.agentops@gmail.com.
